SWARM Wallet is a self-custody wallet for SWARM Mainnet, the live SWARM network. The SWM in it is real, and a confirmed transaction cannot be reversed. The app keeps wallet keys only on the device. By default it connects to the SWARM mainnet wallet server at https://lwd-main.swarm.green:443 to sync blocks and send transactions; the server can be changed in Settings. While a wallet is open it also reads the SWM price from https://wallet.swarm.green; that request carries no address and no balance, and a switch in Settings stops it. The app requires no account, no sign-in and no personal data.

How to test
1. Open the app, accept the risk notice, and create a new wallet. Approve the Face ID or device passcode request. Keep the app open until wallet creation finishes; the first sync of a new wallet can take a few minutes.
2. Open Receive to see the shielded address (it starts with swm1) and its QR code. Copy the address, or scan the QR code with another SWARM wallet.
3. To test sending, transfer a small real amount of SWM (for example 0.01 SWM) to this address from another SWARM Mainnet wallet and wait until the balance becomes spendable. Open Send, enter or scan a SWARM Mainnet address (swm1...), enter an amount, review the fee and confirm.
4. Open History to see the transaction and the balance. The options panel offers the recovery phrase, protected by Face ID or the device passcode.
5. Open Settings to see the wallet server (https://lwd-main.swarm.green:443 by default) and the switch for the SWM price display.

The app starts with an empty wallet. Every screen, the receive address and QR scanning can be reviewed without funds; a funded send needs a small amount of SWM from another SWARM Mainnet wallet. The app contains no miner and offers no purchase, sale, exchange or swap of SWM. It has no ads and no referral or reward programme. Camera access is used only to scan QR codes. Face ID or the device passcode protects local wallet access and the recovery phrase.
