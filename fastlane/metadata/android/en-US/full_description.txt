SWARM Wallet is the phone wallet for SWARM Mainnet, the live network of SWARM: private, proof-of-work money run by its community.

THE COINS HERE ARE REAL. SWM on SWARM Mainnet has real value, and what you lose is lost for good. Nothing in this app is an offer, an investment or a way to earn money. Nobody sells SWM to you through this app, and nobody promises a price.

What you can do
• Create a wallet on your phone. Your recovery words stay with you; nobody else has them.
• Receive SWM at a shielded address (swm1…) and show it as a QR code.
• Send SWM to another SWARM address, with an optional encrypted memo.
• See your balance and your transaction history.
• Lock the app with your phone's screen lock or fingerprint.
• Switch to SWARM Testnet (engineering) in Settings if you want to try things out. Its coins have no value and never will.

What this app does not do
• It does not mine. Phones do not mine SWARM; mining runs on a computer with the separate SWARM Node program.
• It does not hold your coins for you. It is self-custodial: your keys are generated and kept on your phone, and your recovery words are the only backup.
• It has no accounts, no sign-up, no exchange, no swap, no fiat prices, no ads and no referral or reward programme.

Privacy
The app has no analytics and no tracking. It talks to one server to sync blocks and send transactions: the SWARM mainnet wallet server (lwd-main.swarm.green) or a server you choose yourself. Shielded transactions hide sender, receiver and amount on the chain, but they do not hide your internet address from that server, and transparent addresses are public. The camera is used only to read QR codes on your phone. Fingerprint or screen-lock unlock is handled by Android and never leaves the device. Details: https://swarm.green/wallet/privacy/

Open source
SWARM Wallet is a fork of Zingo, an MIT-licensed wallet, adapted for SWARM. Source code: https://github.com/Swarm-Official/swarm-mobile
