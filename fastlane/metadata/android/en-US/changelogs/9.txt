SWARM Wallet runs on SWARM Mainnet, the live network, restarted on 2 October 2026. A wallet made before the restart is moved to the new chain automatically the first time it opens online: the recovery phrase and addresses stay the same, balances start again from the new chain, and a backup of the old wallet file stays on the phone. New: the SWM price with its change and a chart; switch it off in Settings. Unlock with your fingerprint or screen lock.
